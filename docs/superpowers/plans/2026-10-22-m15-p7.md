## Rulings (bounded choices the spec leaves open — overturn any in review)

1. **"?" form:** native `popover`, not `<details>`. `Help({ label, children })` renders
   `<button type="button" className="help" popoverTarget={id} aria-label={label}><span aria-hidden="true">?</span></button>`
   followed by `<span id={id} popover="auto" className="help-pop" role="note">{children}</span>` (`id` from `useId`;
   a `span` so it is valid inside the `p.strip`; children are text only). The browser centres it in the top layer,
   so nothing positions it and nothing overflows at 375 px; Esc and tap-outside close it. The button is a 44 px
   target around a 22 px `--surface-2` circle. No close button, no motion of its own (the existing popover drop-in
   rule may apply; reduced motion as always).
2. **Where "?" goes (t321)** — four spots, three texts, all in `components/Help.tsx`:
   - `SharesHelp({ settings })` in the Buy shares card's `.head`, after the `h2` (label "How buying shares works"):
     shares, credits and the repeat chip in one place.
   - `FactorHelp({ settings })` in the pick card's head (label "What the × means") and on the team page beside the
     next-game line: the × factor, tired and earlier tournaments.
   - `VerifiedHelp({ hours })` (label "What unofficial and verified mean") beside the "Unofficial" pill in the League
     matchup strip, at the end of the Stats live strip, and as the first child of the Stats "All stats" fold (it
     explains its Not verified / Verified / Locked pills; a button can't sit in a `summary`).
   - Credits page and game cards: none (the Credits page already carries the terms; game cards are one tap from the
     matchup's help). Overturnable.
3. **Help copy (exact; `S` = settings, numbers through the Rules page's `n()` rounding — export it from `Help.tsx`
   and have Rules import it):**
   - Shares: "Put credits on players to buy shares in them. A player has {S.min_shares} shares, or one per credit if
     the league puts more on them. You own your credits' worth and score that part of their points. Every team gets
     free credits before each tournament, and unspent ones carry over. A × by a name means you started them in an
     earlier tournament, so their points count less for you."
   - Factor: "The × by a player is what their points count for you this game: your share × tired × earlier
     tournaments. Tired: {tired}. Earlier tournaments: a little less for each one you started them in, never below
     ×{n(S.decay_floor)}. Tap a × to see its parts." where `{tired}` joins `S.tiredness_multipliers` as
     "×0.5 if they started the game before, ×0.75 if 2 games before" (index 0 "the game before", index i "i+1 games
     before"); an empty list reads "never".
   - Verified: "Keepers tap stats during the game, then a keeper checks and verifies them. Until then scores are
     unofficial and can change. Verified stats count; they can be reopened for {S.stat_lock_hours} hours, then
     they're locked."
4. **First-visit guide (t320):** `GuideSteps({ picks })` renders `<ol className="guide-steps">` with exactly:
   1. "**Get a team.** Join or create a league. Your team plays in it all season."
   2. "**Buy shares.** Before each tournament, put your free credits on teammates. You score the part of each player you own."
   3. "**Pick {picks} each game.** Before every game, choose {picks} of your players to start."
   4. "**Higher score wins.** Your starters' stats add up to your score. Win to climb the standings."
   `Guide({ uid })` shows on League (HomePage) for every signed-in user, above everything else on the page, until
   "Got it": an `<aside className="notice guide" aria-label="How it works">` with `<h2>How it works</h2>`, the steps,
   then a `.row` with a `<button type="button">Got it</button>` and a `.more` link "All the rules" to `/rules`.
   `picks` = `loadCurrentSeason()`'s `picks_per_game`, `DEFAULT_SETTINGS.picks_per_game` until it loads or without a
   season. Remembered per account and device: key `` `tribe.guide.${uid}` `` = `'1'` (the `tribe.` prefix); a storage
   read that throws shows the guide, a write that throws hides it for this visit only. Existing players see it once
   too (one tap). **Reopen** = Rules always opens with the same steps (Ruling 10); no "show again" button.
5. **Buy-list form (t322):** `lastTournamentForm(y, excludeStageId)` picks the **last tournament** = the stage other
   than `excludeStageId` that has at least one `final` game in `y.result.games`, latest `y.stageDates` `starts_on`
   (ties by stage id). **Per game** = each athlete's base points (`rawScore(stats, y.settings.stat_weights)`) summed
   over that stage's final games' stat lines, divided by the number of its final games (a game without their line
   counts 0, as the scorer's `absent_score` does). Athletes with no line in any of those games get no entry (shows
   nothing: a new or absent player looks the same, and a blank is calmer than "0"). On the buy list: a
   `<p className="hint">Points a game at {name}, the last tournament. Tap a name for game by game.</p>` above the
   rows; each name becomes `<Link className="title" to={`/player/${id}?t=${stageId}`}>`; under it, when present,
   `<small>{fmt(perGame)} a game</small>`. No last tournament (the live season's first) → today's list unchanged.
   Uses the tournament `WithTournament` already loads (no new load); its failure drops the line, as it drops chips.
