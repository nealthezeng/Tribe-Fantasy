## Rulings (bounded choices the spec leaves open — overturn any in review)

1. **The closed shares card goes away** (amendment 2's "until P3"). Team pages replace "Every team" (each player's
   share is inside the × popover); your own players are on the pick card and your own team page. `AuctionCard` keeps
   only the open buy card: its `show` and `joinedAt` props, the closed `<details>` branch, `collapseSlots` and the
   slots/teams fetches are deleted with their tests. Side effect: between "buying closed" and "shares settled" nothing
   about buying shows (P7 t323 owns that wording).
2. **Which names link to `/team/:membershipId`:** Leaderboards standings rows (your league's view; in the all-leagues
   view only your own league's rows, since `roster_slots` RLS hides other leagues' players), past-tournament rows, both
   names in the League matchup headline, and "You play **X**" on the pick card. Game cards don't (their summary is a
   toggle). Links look like the text around them (inherit colour, underline on hover/focus).
3. **Team page between tournaments** (no next game: `result.next` null): "No tournament on. Players show once the next
   tournament's shares are settled." A team with no roster for the next game (joined late, bought nothing): "No
   players this tournament." A removed team: its "(removed)" name and "No players." Players are listed A→Z.
4. **Who can open a team page:** members of that team's league (current season) and staff (admins, keepers). Anyone
   else, a typed id of another league, or an unknown id: "You can only see teams in your league." with a link to
   Leaderboards. No admin-only extras on the page: admins already rename and edit rosters in Admin → Leagues (M14).
5. **Past tournaments** = every stage of the season with at least one started game, except the stage of
   `result.next` (the one being played), newest first. Each is a `<details>` (name + dates) holding a ranked list:
   each team that played in it, its W–L(–T) there and the standings points its counted games gave (sum of `delta`,
   byes included), ties broken by total score as in Standings. A tournament with games still waiting on stats shows
   a "Waiting on stats" pill and counts only final games. No per-game list: the League tab's Games list has them.
6. **Owner rename** = new RPC `rename_my_team(p_membership, p_name)`: the caller's own, not-removed team only
   (`NOT_MEMBER` otherwise, also for admins on others' teams: they use `admin_rename_team`); allowed any time, mid-tournament
   too (a name is display only). Audit action `rename_my_team` with `{old, new}`; History shows "Renamed team old → new".
   UI: first thing in League settings, which now always renders for your team. Undo = rename back (no confirm).
7. **Tabs on deep routes:** `/stats/*`, `/player/*`, `/tally` light **Stats**; `/team/*` lights **Leaderboards**;
   Me/Credits/Rules/Admin light none (as today).
8. **`fmt` and `record` move to `src/app/lib/tournament.ts`** (next to `teamLabel`); `TournamentCard`,
   `LeaderboardsPage` and `admin/LeagueBrowser` import them from there (the LeagueBrowser copy is deleted).
9. **Admin CSV before the first tournament:** with a season and no tournament, Stats shows admins the same "Download
   CSV" button under "No tournaments yet."
