makes the League tab your matchup and moves standings to a new Leaderboards tab.

only: one new page, owner names added to the league loader, tabs reshuffled. P0 and P1 ship separately (tags
`m15-p0`, `m15-p1`), P0 first. P2–P9 get their own plans when they start, against the code as it stands then.
